# Rallycademy data shortlist (25 September 2026)
Research status: no free, current and expressly rights-cleared full stack found. This is a shortlist for negotiation and testing, not approval to ingest or publish. Market-value estimates and transfer fees are different targets.

| Source | Feature / label fit | Rights and use status | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wyscout authors' Figshare soccer-logs (2017/18 PL and other leagues) | Player birthDate and position; event IDs and match lineup/substitution data may derive minutes/goals/assists. No value labels or live season | Players and Events items display CC BY 4.0 with citation; Events download page flags restrictions. Confirm Matches and all relevant item licences, access and age coverage first | Best no-cost historical **feature** research lead, not the product feed or a complete model |
| StatsBomb Open Data | Selected competitions, event/lineup JSON, historical only; DOB and U21 cohort sufficiency to validate. | API-Football free | Player features and DOB look plausible in advertised endpoints; exact league-season field fill remains untested. No market values | $0, 100 requests/day; fantasy-style use mentioned but upstream competition/data rights still customer responsibility | Trial only if ordinary signup accessible; no bot-wall bypass |
| Sportmonks | Paid player stats and historic feeds; no valuation-label product established | Plans start €29/month and older history may be extra; contract for training, display, competition rights needed | Paid stats lead, flag before committing |
| Off The Pitch | Modelled valuation history and monthly updates, 10,000+ players / 34 leagues advertised; not raw transfer fees | Ordinary terms allow personal noncommercial use only and prohibit automated/systematic/derivative/commercial reuse without prior written consent, despite API marketing | Contact for bespoke licence only, never scrape |
| Football Benchmark | Four/five valuation dates/season, 27 leagues, but 450+ minutes in preceding 365 days excludes many U21 prospects | Public methodology, no grant found for training/display or historical bulk export; subscription/licence inquiry needed | Check coverage bias and written rights before negotiation |
| Transfermarkt-derived Kaggle data | Features + historical labels but paused updates mid-2026 | Upstream terms prohibit automated copying and ML training; uploader CC0 label doesn't clear source | Do not train, ingest or publish |
| StatFootDB (Figshare) | Recent big-five player stats, no licensed value labels | Dataset says it scraped FotMob for noncommercial academic research; underlying commercial/ML rights unresolved | Do not use for Rally without source permission |

## Ask a prospective vendor before using data
1. Is Rally's use allowed: commercial consumer app, fantasy/league play, minors' public sports data, and per-player redisplay? Which competitions, seasons and territories are sublicensed?
2. Can Rally store historical snapshots, combine IDs with another vendor, train and retrain regression, publish derived player-specific forecasts/history and retain trained weights after cancellation? Are there source-credit and takedown obligations?
3. Can we get a sample of U21 players at 16, 17, 18, 19, 20, 21 with DOB, match minutes/goals/assists, dated values and corrections, including low-minute players? What is the exact label definition, timestamp and known gaps?
4. What are the cost, included usage, non-production trial rights, expected correction delay and usage limits? No plan purchase before the owner approves total and terms.

**Bottom line:** A historical feature pipeline may be prototyped from item-licensed Wyscout data after access/licence checks; it cannot validate the proposed transfer-value predictor without a lawful label history. The only viable current-market valuation suppliers identified here require written, purpose-specific contracts. A synthetic UI demonstrates interaction, not predictive quality. Keep the fictional Rally preview clearly labelled, with no live provider claim.
