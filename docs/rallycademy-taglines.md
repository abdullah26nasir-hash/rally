# Rallycademy tagline options (private copy study)

The feature name is Rallycademy. These lines position it as following real 16-21 footballers and recording an early call, not as a football school, a talent agency or a live-data predictor today. Keep fictional preview copy separate until licensed data and a validated model exist.

**Recommended:** "Spot them before the stands do." Short, football-native, solo discovery and later social bragging in one sentence. It is an invitation, not a claim of predictive accuracy.

Alternates:
- "Your eye for who's next." Personal watchlist first; less explicit about proof.
- "The next name starts here." Good broad discovery line, but could imply Rally launched the player's career. Use with caution.
- "See the rise. Keep the receipt." Strong link to forecast movement and Rally's timestamped early-call artifact; use only where a movement chart truly exists, and don't suggest a model is live in the fictional preview.
- "Follow the talent. Prove you were early." Most literal for product onboarding; longer but tells the whole loop.
- "Call it before the crowd." Dry, confident football-fan tone; the object of "it" needs supporting UI context.

Avoid "academy for future stars", "discover the next Messi", "learn to scout", "the future of football talent". The first resembles EA's promotion; the second overpromises; the third sounds like a training product and reinforces Khan Academy confusion; the fourth is generic. None of these candidates has been trademark or ad-claim cleared. Final public copy should match actual product capability and data rights.
