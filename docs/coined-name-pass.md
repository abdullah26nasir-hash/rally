# Coined-name pass for the Rally feature (25 September 2026)

Rallycademy remains the working name. This is a fresh coined-name study; nothing here is a final pick or a cleared mark.

| Candidate | Say it | Memory hook | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Scouvela** | skoo-VEL-a | Scout + reveal: find talent early, track the story | Similar unrelated Scouva/Scouval names; may need teaching |
| **Ballivo** | bal-EE-vo | Ball + vivo: live growth | Weak scouting meaning, could be mistaken for a surname |
| **Pitcharo** | PITCH-a-ro | Pitch + arrow, football and upward trajectory | Spelling/pronunciation and player-name feel |
| **Scoutella** | skow-TELL-a | Scout + tell: a prospect's story | Sounds like a food brand; less distinct |

A quick public-web exact-name screen did not show a direct football app for these four. This is weak negative evidence, not ownership or trademark clearance. Rejected in this pass: Scoutra (existing app), Scoutiva (football scouting agency), Pitchora (football scores app), Rallik (marketing software), Rallio (AI app), Rallista (driving app). Candidate names should be tested aloud and against official mark registers, domains, app stores, similar sounds and relevant jurisdictions before any public rename.

The useful pattern from iconic names is not adding a z for its own sake. **Google** drew on the mathematical googol, fitting massive information indexing; Google's own account and a first-hand Stanford recollection agree on that core, while the latter says its spelling came from a domain search typo: https://about.google/company-info/our-story/ and https://graphics.stanford.edu/~dk/google_name_origin.html . **Hulu's** archived company FAQ describes a short playful sound with a Mandarin gourd or vessel association: https://web.archive.org/web/20121005040222/www.hulu.com/about/media_faq . **Zillow's** founders say they blended zillion (data) with pillow (home), with a name flexible beyond one service: https://www.geekwire.com/2016/zillow-10-years/ . A secondary report attributes Spotify's name to a misheard shout during founder brainstorming, rather than its later "spot + identify" explanation: https://www.howtogeek.com/821019/why-is-it-called-spotify/ . **Netflix** visibly combines net + flicks, but I did not find a primary naming-origin account, so don't present an origin anecdote as verified.

Official UK trademark lookup: https://www.gov.uk/search-for-trademark . None of this approves a public name or changes the current fictional preview.
