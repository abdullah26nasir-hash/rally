# Rallycademy transfer-value PoC, 25 Sep 2026

## Decision
Do not switch the live game or call the model real-data-backed yet. Target age is 16-21 at the season cutoff (the user's selected range, not proof of any third-party competition's rules). The Instagram reel is a coding-project recipe, not evidence that its data is free or that it can be used to train a model. It proposes linear regression on goals, assists, minutes, age and position to predict a player's transfer-market value; compare the prediction with actual value. It never identifies a licensed source for the target values.

## Source checks
- Reel: https://www.instagram.com/reels/DckAmf2hdrt/ (sujar.tech, 1:51). Narration explicitly says Python requests + football-data.org, linear regression, features goals/assists/minutes/age/position; stack pandas, scikit-learn, matplotlib. Random forest/XGBoost is a separate match-outcome project, not the transfer-value model.
- football-data.org current registration page explicitly calls free access "Basic data (Fixtures, Results and League Tables)" and says paid tiers have more data: https://www.football-data.org/client/register . Pricing says €0, 12 competitions, 10 calls/minute; lineups/scorers/squads are in deeper tiers: https://www.football-data.org/pricing . Anonymous live checks: `/v4/competitions/` HTTP 200; PL finished matches, PL scorers and player/person detail HTTP 403 without a key. Anonymous 403 is not a free-account trial, but the operator's tier descriptions do not support the reel's claim of free player-level stats. Person schema can contain DOB, aggregated minutes, goals and assists when authorised: https://docs.football-data.org/general/v4/person.html . It does not offer actual transfer-value labels in that schema.
- API-Football terms explicitly allow fantasy-game use as an application class but do not grant publication/competition rights, leaving clearance with the builder: https://api-sports.io/terms . Free $0 tier is 100 calls/day, recent seasons, listed player/fixture endpoints: https://www.api-football.com/pricing . Dashboard blocked on a persistent Cloudflare verification wall; no account, key or calls were made, so target PL 2026 season player-minute/assist/DOB completeness is unverified.
- A public Transfermarkt-derived Kaggle dataset joins matches, appearances and valuation labels: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/davidcariboo/player-scores . But upstream collection is paused, with appearances ending 28 June 2026 and valuations 12 June 2026: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dcaribou/transfermarkt-datasets/master/README.md . Transfermarkt's current terms prohibit automated copying and using site digital content to train or develop ML/AI (section 11.1): https://www.transfermarkt.co.uk/intern/anb . A dataset uploader's CC0 tag does not settle upstream rights. No download, training or public reuse occurred.

## A technical experiment that would answer the question, after rights clearance
1. Agree on a lawful source for dated player appearances, date of birth and dated historical transfer-market valuations or transaction fees. Keep transfers (real fees) distinct from subjective estimates (market values). Obtain written permission for model training and display, including under-18 data treatment and competition marks. No scraping rights-restricted sites.
2. Define a precise prediction moment: end of each season or a monthly snapshot; age 16-21 as of that date. Freeze player features as of that date and predict valuation at a later date, e.g. 6 or 12 months. Only past goals/assists/minutes and club/position available at that moment may enter the features; never leak future values or outcomes.
3. Baseline: a simple median by age/position and previous value if licensed. Then fit a scikit-learn `ColumnTransformer` + `OneHotEncoder` + `LinearRegression` on log(1 + value). Compare on later calendar seasons and club/player-disjoint validation, not a random appearance-row split. Report MAE, median absolute percentage error (when labels nonzero), sample sizes and confidence intervals by age bucket (16-18, 19-21), minutes band, position and club. If label sparsity or bias makes it unreliable, stop rather than fabricate a recommendation.
4. Decouple product scoring from transfer valuation: current Rally points come from appearances and receipts; a value forecast is a separate optional signal, not a real transfer fee or a promise. Rank only under clear data licences and publish model date, target definition, and uncertainty. No money/price vocabulary in the current preview copy until the product decision changes.

## Status and recommendation
The free football-data.org feed is suitable for a separate fixtures/results ingest PoC after registration, but cannot alone feed the proposed player-value model or the current Rally player-level scoring. API-Football can be tested for player stats when a legitimate free account is accessible; it still lacks a sourced valuation target. The real-data PoC is blocked on rights-cleared labels. No vendor account was created and nothing was deployed.
