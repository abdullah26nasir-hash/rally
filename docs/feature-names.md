# Rally feature-name candidates (25 September 2026)

Current working feature name: **Rally Wonderkids**, selected after the September 25 naming discussion; Rallycademy was the prior working choice. Formal trademark, domain and app-store clearance remains pending before public launch. Earlier alternatives below are retained as decision history.

Earlier working recommendation: **Rally First Call**. It is football-fan language for spotting a player early, matches the timestamped scout receipt and fits both a private player-following habit and league bragging rights. UI labels can stay plain: "Your First Calls" for followed players, "First Call" on a receipt, "Forecast" for the model chart. This is **not trademark clearance**: the phrase is ordinary and may be used by others.

| Candidate | Fit | Collision / caution |
|---|---|---|
| Rally First Call | Strongest across solo tracking, dated prediction changes and proof of an early pick | Generic words, formal mark clearance needed |
| Rally Scout Sheet | Scout's working paper, well matched to the follow feed | "Scout sheet" is common for sports forms; weaker distinction |
| Rally Scoutmark | Coined, stamp/proof association; strong visual identity | "ScoutMark" exists outside football, check adjacent classes |
| Rally Prospect Ledger | Dated record of player progress | "Ledger" sounds financial; conflicts with current no-money UI tone |
| Rally Formline | Compact, conveys a plotted form trend | Fitness uses Formline; scouting link less clear |

Avoid **Rally Next XI** (https://nextxi.app/planner), **Rally Rising XI** (https://www.pennrisingxi.com/faq), **Rally Scoutbook** (https://scoutbookverified.com/), **Rally First Look** (https://www.1stlooksports.org/), **Rally On the Radar** (https://otr-sports.com/otr-recruit), **Rally The Watchlist** (https://thewatchlist.app/en), and **Rally First Team** (https://www.footballclubmanagement.co.uk/firstteammanager): direct or near-direct product/category collisions from a quick search. "Future Stars" belongs to EA's current FC promotion; avoid it even with a Rally prefix as the final public name. Wonderkids, Next Up, Radar, Breakout and Class are familiar but broadly used in football; descriptive or too generic for a distinctive feature.

Screen method: public-web exact phrase and relevant unprefixed football-app queries, September 25, 2026. No official database search, domain availability check, app-store search, legal view or guarantee of uniqueness. Before shipping a mark, check UK IPO (https://www.gov.uk/search-for-trademark), relevant other jurisdictions, similar spellings and classes, and domains/social handles. All names are provisional.

## Football-term pass
- **Rally First Call** stays the recommended feature name. "Call" is a scout's judgment and "first" makes the early-pick receipt meaningful, but the phrase itself is generic. This is distinct from renaming a metric or a single screen.
- **Rally Academy Watch** is a grounded fallback for the solo feed, but not every 16-21-year-old eligible player remains in an academy, so it could misdescribe the player pool. Better as a content category than the whole feature.
- **Rally Homegrown** is recognisable UK football language, but "homegrown" has a formal league eligibility meaning unrelated to age (and could falsely imply every tracked player qualifies). A Homegrown Sports player app exists: https://homegrownsports.app/ . Avoid as the whole product.
- **Rally Youth Intake** evokes Football Manager's newly generated academy cohort, not real 16-21 players already in senior football. Its official editorial uses the term: https://www.footballmanager.com/the-dugout/developing-and-maximising-your-youth-intakes-fm26 . Avoid.
- **Rally Newgens** names fictional regenerated players in Football Manager culture, the wrong signal for real footballers; a UK NEWGEN application appears in a third-party search result, unverified. Avoid.
- **Rally La Masia** is Barcelona's real academy identity: https://www.fcbarcelona.com/en/club/identity/la-masia . Do not use.
- **Rally Academy** is too broad and suggests a training institution rather than a forecast/watchlist. **Rally Breakthrough XI** sounds football-native, but limiting the feature to eleven players conflicts with solo following and five-player picks. 

## Wordplay pass
**Rallycademy** is the most pronounceable academy portmanteau, without a direct exact-phrase result in a quick web search. It is playful, but may make a player tracker sound like an educational course. "Rally Academy" itself is already used for an unrelated forex school (https://www.rally.academy/) and motorsport academy (https://www.mirallyacademy.ie/), so the blend still needs formal clearance. "Rally Academy Watch" works better as a label for academy-specific players than the full 16-21 product, since many eligible players have reached senior squads. Avoid "Rally Grounds": search results prominently refer to Nazi Party rally grounds. "Rally First Whistle" collides with an existing football management app (https://my.firstwhistle.app/); "Rally Call-Up" is close to an existing app (https://www.thecallup.app/); "Rally Ranks" has an existing app (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/rally-rank/id6744039160). The owner chose Rallycademy after this screen; its course-like reading remains a copy/design consideration, not a veto.

## User-proposed "Future Ballerz" collision screen (25 September 2026)
Do **not** adopt the exact spelling: an active youth-football club in Enfield already trades as Future Ballerz FC and Future Ballerz Lab, promotes 2026/27 trials and owns https://futureballerz.com/ . Closely spelled youth-football coaching brands also exist at https://future-ballers.co.uk/ (Kent) and https://futureballers.se/ (Sweden). Prefixing with Rally would not erase the same-market, same-audience phrase collision. This concern is stronger than EA's "Future Stars" issue: the word "Future" alone is broad, but the full "Future Ballerz" combination is already in use. No social handle availability or trademark registration was established; search results cannot prove availability. Rally Wonderkids is descriptive football language with many related uses and still needs formal clearance, while Rallycademy was the working name at that point; see current choice above.

## Wonder Kids / Ballerz / Ballers screen (25 September 2026)
"Ballerz" is already an operating UK football experience brand, https://ballerz.co.uk/ (its own page displays Ballerz®), with youth programmes, player statistics and ranking experiences. Its launch and footballer backers are described by its landlord: https://www.landsec.com/en/media-insights/press-releases/landsec-continues-grow-leisure-offer-bluewater-rio-ferdinand-backed . The Rally prefix does not automatically cure that same-sector overlap. "Ballers" is also HBO's series title and, more directly for digital football, Ballers App: Football Training exists at https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=se.selfmadeacademy . "Wonder Kids"/"Wonderkids" is a familiar football term rather than a uniquely coined mark; FM Wonderkids exists at https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=tom.groven.wunderkidz . "Rally Wonderkids" is more differentiated than an unprefixed name but would still need trademark, domain and app-store clearance. None of these checks replaces a legal mark search. Rallycademy was the working name at that point; see current choice above.

## Standalone wonderkid spelling screen (25 September 2026)
**Wunderkidz** is a direct UK football-game collision: UK Games Fund describes a youth-academy game by that name, https://www.ukgamesfund.com/funded-project/wunderkidz/ , and its current App Store page includes scouting, player development and tournaments, https://apps.apple.com/us/app/wunderkidz/id1535218623 . **Wunderkids** differs only at the end and reads almost the same; not a clean escape. **Wonderkidz** already identifies a UK children's-education business, https://www.wonderkidz.co.uk/ , and the o/z change alone does not make a football brand ownable. Standalone **Wonder Kids/Wonderkids** also has an FM Wonderkids app at https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=tom.groven.wunderkidz . No spelling is cleared or recommended unprefixed. The current working name remains **Rally Wonderkids** until the owner changes it, with formal trademark review required before launch.
